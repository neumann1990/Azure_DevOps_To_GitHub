## Published abstract

> **Azure DevOps to GitHub Enterprise: The Great Migration**
>
> Ready to embark on the quest of moving from Azure DevOps to GitHub Enterprise? Think of this session as your comedic survival guide—part tech talk, part group therapy, and part "Why did we set up our pipelines like that?"
>
> We'll walk through the real-world journey of migration with equal parts practicality and humor. You'll hear stories, lessons learned, and a few confessions from teams who've already survived the trek. Along the way, we'll explore:
>
> - The real reasons teams migrate (yes, AI is one of them)
> - Which parts of Azure DevOps will pack their bags gracefully… and which will cling to the doorframe
> - How to rebuild your pipelines and workflows in GitHub without needing a three-week meditation retreat
> - Change-management tips so your team transitions smoothly rather than forming a small, tool-related uprising
>
> Whether you're planning a migration or just here for the schadenfreude of other people's configuration woes, you'll leave with practical strategies, a few good laughs, and a renewed appreciation for the automation that keeps the SDLC wheels turning.
>
> Join us—it'll be informative, mildly cathartic, and far less painful than rewriting that one pipeline you've been avoiding.

The four bullets are the promises. Each maps to a section of the deck — see [`02-narrative-and-theme.md`](02-narrative-and-theme.md).

## What the presentation is *not*

> **Kevin:** Two debates I'm deliberately not having on stage.

- A debate on the best CI/CD platform (*"why GitHub and not X?"*)
- A debate on the best AI tool (*"why Copilot and not X?"*)

The talk assumes the destination is already chosen. It's about the journey, not the argument.
